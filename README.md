## Overview

**SEI ONE** (Synthetic Environment Infrastructure ONE) defines the deterministic specification for embedding high-fidelity synthetic fashion entities into containerized and industrial architectural environments. Designed by **DPRLAB**, this open specification unifies spatial camera geometry, dusk-to-dawn lighting remapping, and extreme material juxtaposition into a standardized schema.

By formalizing the translation layer between generative spatial models and architectural R&D, SEI ONE establishes a reproducible, open benchmark for spatial visualization and synthetic entity synthesis.

---

## Core Specification Architecture

The SEI ONE framework operates across three key synchronization axes:

1. **Spatial Scale & Lens Geometry (`/spec/geometry.json`)**
   - Perspective alignment forcing low-camera elevation angles (15°–35° upward tilt).
   - Focal depth mapping to enforce foreground entity dominance against cantilevered structural overhangs.

2. **Specular & Ambient Lighting Remapping (`/spec/lighting.yaml`)**
   - Kelvin temperature balance bridging 3000K warm interior/stairwell LED channels with cool (6500K–8000K) twilight ambient sky fill.
   - Dynamic reflection mapping onto high-gloss synthetic polymers (e.g., latex, smoked glass, anodized steel).

3. **Material Juxtaposition Matrix (`/spec/materials.json`)**
   - Precise texture friction parameters between raw cast-concrete plinths, western red cedar, corrugated steel, and synthetic human topologies.
